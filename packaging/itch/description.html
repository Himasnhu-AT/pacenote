A rally festival in one open valley, where the road is read to you.

Drive anywhere in a 4 × 4 km valley: gravel switchbacks, forest roads and the town of Fernhollow. Pull up at an event marker to race point-to-point routes against the clock or five AI rivals while your co-driver calls the corners: "left four into right two, don't cut, crest."

What's in this build (v0.2.0)

  • An open world: 21 roads (about 38 km), a town, traffic, a day and night clock, a minimap and a full map with route planning.
  • 5 events: 2 rallies and 3 sprints. Run each as a Time Trial against your ghost, or a Race against 5 rivals at Rookie, Pro, Ace or Legend.
  • 13 roadside challenges: radar posts, pace sections, slide sections and jump boards, rated 1 to 3 stars.
  • A career: earn Notes and xp, chain style skills into a Rhythm bonus, and buy and upgrade cars at the Service Park. There are 8 cars and 7 upgrade parts, and the upgrades change the physics, not just a stat bar.
  • Driving that feels earned: weight transfer, gravel slides, crests that go light, and damage to engine, steering and suspension. Optional traction control and ABS.
  • Pace notes graded 1 to 6 the way real co-drivers grade them, taken from each road's actual curvature.

New in v0.2.0

  • The open world replaces the old stage menu.
  • Rivals race harder: they push for passes, follow closer and rarely let you by.
  • Rubbing doors with another car no longer spins you round.
  • After a race, Enter takes you back to free roam (Backspace retries).
  • Fixed a crash when abandoning an event.

Controls

  • W / S throttle and brake (hold S to reverse), A / D steer, Space handbrake
  • C camera view, R recover onto the road (+5 s), M map, Esc pause
  • At an event marker: 1-4 difficulty, Enter race, T time trial
  • Keyboard or gamepad, fully rebindable.

Playing together

The original two-player mode (one drives, one reads the map and calls the notes, with built-in voice chat) is still in, on the classic stages. It's direct between two PCs: the driver hosts and the guide joins by address. Bringing it into the open world is next.

System requirements

  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • macOS: macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel (signed and notarized).
  • A microphone for voice chat (optional).

Privacy

The game sends anonymous stats (event, result, where crashes happen) and crash reports to help balance it. No names, chat or voice are ever sent. The first launch tells you, and Settings → Privacy turns either off or deletes your data ("Forget me"). The DO_NOT_TRACK environment variable is respected.
