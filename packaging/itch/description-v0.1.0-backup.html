One of you drives. The other reads the road.

In Pacenote the driver has the wheel, the windscreen and nothing else: no map, no corner arrows. The guide has the stage map, the car's position and a list of every corner ahead, graded from 1 (hairpin) to 6 (flat out), but can't touch the controls. The only way through is to talk: "left four into right two, don't cut, crest."

No partner tonight? Drive solo with the AI co-driver reading the notes.

The free demo

Three of the twenty stages: Pinewater (gravel, pine forest), Thornback (tarmac, scrub) and Frostline (snow). Follow the game here on itch.io to hear when the full game is out.

Features

  • Two players, two roles: driver and guide, on two PCs, with built-in voice chat (push-to-talk or open mic).
  • Pick the weather (clear, overcast, fog, rain) and the time of day (morning to dusk) for every stage.
  • Cockpit, bonnet and chase cameras. In the cockpit, the driver's hands work the wheel, the sequential shifter and the hydraulic handbrake.
  • A 4WD rally car with weight transfer, grip that changes with the surface, handbrake turns and lift-off slides. Optional traction control and ABS.
  • Damage to engine, steering and suspension; a bad enough hit retires the car.
  • Corner calls graded 1 to 6 the way real co-drivers grade them, plus crests and narrows. Choose when the co-driver calls them and how they're phrased.
  • Engine, turbo, gravel and tyre sounds synthesised live from the car's state.
  • Leaderboards and ghost cars per stage and conditions.
  • Keyboard or gamepad, fully rebindable.

Controls

  • W / S throttle and brake (hold S to reverse), A / D steer, Space handbrake
  • C camera view, R recover onto the road (+5 s), N co-driver voice on/off, Esc pause

Playing together

Online play is direct between the two PCs: the driver hosts, the guide joins by address (same network, or the driver forwards a port). There's no matchmaking yet.

System requirements

  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • macOS: macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel (signed and notarized).
  • A microphone for voice chat (optional).

Privacy

The game sends anonymous stats (stage, result, where crashes happen) and crash reports to help balance the stages. No names, chat or voice are ever sent. The first launch tells you, and Settings → Privacy turns either off or deletes your data ("Forget me"). The DO_NOT_TRACK environment variable is respected.
